Learning guide

  1. Study a small group of words and notice how they are used.
  2. Recall the meaning before flipping each flashcard.
  3. Practise meanings in Word–Meaning Match and use context in Context Challenge.
  4. Complete Quiz A, B, and C on separate occasions.
  5. Return to Review My Words for mistakes and scheduled practice.

What the scores mean

XP rewards completed activities. Mastery comes from correct answers across different skills and attempts. Matching reports first-try accuracy; word search reports words found and hints used.

Audio and saving

The lessons, visuals, and games work offline. Natural dictionary recordings need internet access. The app does not use the device’s robotic speech voice; if a recording is temporarily unavailable, the pronunciation symbols remain visible.

Approved students remain signed in on their device and their progress syncs online. Choose Sign out on a shared device. Download a result or a CSV from My Progress to share your work.

Keyboard and display

Use Tab to move between controls and Enter or Space to select. Word search also supports arrow keys, Home, End, Enter, and Escape. Use A− and A+ to change text size. Escape closes a dialog.

Vocabulary definitions and word lists adapted from the supplied learning app. Pronunciation recordings link to online dictionaries; usage corrections were checked against Collins English Dictionary. Students never need the teacher password.
